## Area Chart

An area chart is a data visualization tool based on the line chart, featuring a filled-in area between the axis and the line to emphasize 
**magnitude, volume, and trends over time**. 
It excels at **showing cumulative totals** and how individual components contribute to a whole.

### Key Features & Uses
- **Emphasizes Magnitude:** The shaded area draws attention to the volume of data, making it better than a standard line chart for comparing total quantities.
- **Trend Tracking:** Excellent for showing changes over continuous dimensions, most commonly time.
- **Part-to-Whole Representation:** Stacked area charts are effective for showing how different categories contribute to a cumulative total

An **area chart** displays the magnitude of trends over time for single or **overlapping** data series, shaded below the line. 

A **stacked area chart** plots multiple series vertically, **stacking them** to show both individual category trends and the total cumulative volume. 

Use simple areas to compare magnitude; use stacked to show composition.

#### Key Differences and Use Cases:
- **Area Chart (Overlapping):** Best for showing the trend of multiple metrics over time, where you need to compare values directly against a common baseline (0).
- **Stacked Area Chart:** Ideal for visualizing the total cumulative volume of all categories, as well as how each individual category contributes to that total over time.
