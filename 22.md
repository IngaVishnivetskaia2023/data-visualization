## Bubble Chart

A bubble chart is essentially a scatter plot that displays three (and sometimes four) dimensions of data at once. 
While a standard scatter plot uses `x` and `y` coordinates, a bubble chart **adds the size of the bubble to represent a third variable**.

- **X-Axis (Horizontal):** Represents the first independent or quantitative variable.
- **Y-Axis (Vertical):** Represents the second independent or quantitative variable.
- **Bubble Size (Z-Axis):** Represents the third dimension of data. Crucially, the area of the bubble (not its radius or diameter) should be proportional to the value to avoid visual misinterpretation.
- **Bubble Color:** Often used as a fourth dimension to represent a category (e.g., geographic region) or a separate continuous variable.
