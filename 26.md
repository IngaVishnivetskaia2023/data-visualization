## Waterfall

A waterfall chart (or "bridge chart") displays the cumulative effect of sequential positive and negative values on an initial starting value, 
connecting a start point to a final total. Its key components are anchored start/end columns, floating intermediate change bars, 
and color-coded bars indicating increases, decreases, and totals.
