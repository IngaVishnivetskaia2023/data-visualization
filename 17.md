## Error Bars

Error bars are graphical representations on charts that show **the variability, uncertainty, or precision of data**, 
indicating **the range within which the true value likely lies**. 
Commonly used to represent standard deviation, standard error, or confidence intervals, 
they help visually assess if differences between data points are statistically significant.
