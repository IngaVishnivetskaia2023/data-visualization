## Pie Chart

It’s advisable to remember the below points when adding a pie chart to your presentation design.

- Always mark your data in percentages once you’ve broken it down
- Avoid creating too many data categories. 5 categories are enough and won’t clutter up your pie chart

However, if the numeric values indicate some other statistic where the sum across groups does not equal the statistic ignoring groups, that’s when we run into problems. Examples include taking the average of a metric (e.g. average spend by user type) or if we look at proportions that are independent (e.g. proportion of survey respondents who use each of the polled apps). A bar chart is fine for this case, but a pie chart falls short. 
Since the circular shape implies that slices are parts of a whole, it is too easy for a reader to mistake a sum of slices as a representative of some sort of total.
